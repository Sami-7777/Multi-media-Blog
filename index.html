🎵 Songs

1. Jo Tum Mere Ho – Anuv Jain

Description: "Jo Tum Mere Ho" is a soft and emotional Hindi song by Anuv Jain. The song talks about love, relationships, and the feeling of being close to someone special. Its simple music and meaningful lyrics give it a calm and romantic feel.

2. Tera Mera Rishta – Mustafa Zahid

Description: "Tera Mera Rishta" is an emotional song known for its soulful vocals and strong expression of love. The song describes the deep connection between two people and the memories that remain even when they are apart.

3. Husn – Anuv Jain

Description: "Husn" is a popular romantic song by Anuv Jain. The song describes beauty and attraction through simple but emotional lyrics. Its acoustic sound and peaceful vocals make it a relaxing song to listen to.

4. Iktara – Kavita Seth

Description: "Iktara" is a beautiful Hindi song from the movie Wake Up Sid. The song has a peaceful and dreamy atmosphere and expresses the emotions of falling in love and discovering new feelings.
